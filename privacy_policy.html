Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 14, 2025

ExploreMy ("we", "us", "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what personal information we collect, how we use it, and your choices regarding that information.

3. Sharing & Disclosure

We may share personal information with:

We use cookies and similar technologies for authentication, preferences, analytics and advertising. You can control cookies through your browser settings. Blocking cookies may affect functionality.

5. Security

We implement administrative, technical and physical safeguards to protect your personal information, but no system is 100% secure. If a breach occurs we will follow applicable legal notice requirements.

6. Your Rights & Choices

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or restrict processing of your personal information. To exercise these rights, contact us at privacy@exploremy.example.

7. Data Retention

We retain information as long as necessary to provide Services and for legitimate business purposes, or as required by law.

8. International Transfers

We may transfer information across borders. We will take steps to ensure data is handled in accordance with this Policy and applicable law.

9. Children

Our Services are not directed at children under 13 (or higher age where required). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

10. Changes to This Policy
